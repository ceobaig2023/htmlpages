2 · Main immigration route

Primary route

Express Entry — Federal Skilled Worker

  • At least one year continuous, paid, qualifying skilled work in the same primary NOC within the last 10 years.
  • 1,560 qualifying hours, generally full-time or equivalent part-time.
  • At least CLB 7 in each language ability.
  • Immigration-purpose ECA for foreign education.
  • At least 67/100 FSW selection points.

FSW eligibility is separate from CRS ranking. Reaching 67 points does not guarantee an invitation.

3 · Step-by-step roadmap

01

Confirm IELTS bands and validity

Get the official Test Report Form. Record Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking separately, plus the test date. For IELTS General Training, CLB 9 requires Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0 and Speaking 7.0. An overall 8 does not prove CLB 9. Results must be less than two years old at the relevant Express Entry stages.

IRCC approved language tests and conversion ↗
02

Confirm her actual teaching NOC — do not rely on “Homeroom Teacher” title

Possible NOC 41220 applies if she teaches secondary-school academic subjects and performs duties such as preparing lessons, delivering instruction, setting/marking assessments and evaluating student progress. If she teaches elementary grades, NOC 41221 may be more appropriate. A homeroom title alone is not enough; age/grade taught and day-to-day duties decide the match.

For FSW, select one primary NOC where she can prove one continuous year of paid qualifying work. For Education category selection, the eligible-occupation experience can be accumulated over the required period and need not be continuous.

Official NOC search ↗ · NOC 41220 duties and requirements ↗
03

Request a detailed YIS employment reference letter

Ask HR for a signed letter on official letterhead with employer address/contact, job title, exact start/end dates, employment status, paid salary, hours per week and detailed duties. Gather contract, appointment letter, payslips, salary bank credits and any relieving/experience certificate. If she is still employed, request a current letter showing start date and ongoing status.

Check that the dates and hours establish at least 1,560 qualifying hours and, for FSW, a continuous 12-month period in the same primary NOC. Do not inflate hours or duties.

04

Order ECA assessments for BSc and B.Ed

Use an IRCC-designated ECA organisation. If claiming FSW points for two or more credentials, each credential being claimed needs an assessment and at least one must be a programme of three years or longer. The ECA—not the degree title—determines Canadian equivalency. Ask the assessor how to send transcripts directly from each institution.

Calculate FSW points out of 100

Illustrative scenario only: age 22 = 12; two qualifying credentials = 22; CLB 9+ in all four abilities = 24; one year qualifying experience = 9; arranged employment = 0; adaptability = 0. Total = 67/100. If any assumption is not met, her score may fall below the FSW threshold. Confirm with actual ECA, IELTS bands and employment evidence.

06

Calculate CRS using the current 2026 rules

Do not rely on the earlier rough CRS estimate of 446. IRCC changed CRS scoring effective November 3, 2026, including a new labour-market integration factor and changes to job-offer/Canadian-work scoring. Use the official live calculator and the score shown in her IRCC profile after entering verified details. Her exact CRS cannot be finalised until ECA and IELTS bands are known.

07

Check Education category eligibility

Current Education category occupations include secondary school teachers (NOC 41220), elementary and kindergarten teachers (41221), early childhood educators (42202), instructors of persons with disabilities (42203), and teacher assistants (43100). The category requires at least 12 months full-time equivalent experience in one listed occupation in the preceding three years; it can be in Canada or abroad and need not be continuous. She must also qualify for an Express Entry programme and rank high enough in a category round.

Current category criteria and occupation list ↗
08

Prepare settlement funds

For one applicant, IRCC currently lists CAD $15,263. Keep it legally accessible, unborrowed and available at application and when the PR visa is issued. Obtain a bank letter on official letterhead showing account numbers, opening dates, current balances, six-month average balances, bank contact details and outstanding debts. Explain large recent deposits with source documents. Property equity and borrowed money do not qualify.

IRCC proof-of-funds rules ↗
09

Create Express Entry profile once eligible

Create an IRCC secure account and enter accurate personal, education, language, work, NOC, ECA and funds details. Select intended provinces outside Quebec if applying through FSW. Save confirmation details and monitor profile expiry, draw rounds and any requests to update funds or information. A profile is not the PR application.

Create an Express Entry profile ↗ · Invitation rounds ↗
10

Explore provincial nomination and Canadian employment

Research provincial nominee streams that accept overseas teachers or skilled workers. A nomination may add substantial CRS points, but each province has its own occupation, job-offer, connection and intake rules. A Canadian teaching job may require provincial teacher certification; immigration NOC eligibility does not grant a licence to teach in a public school.

11

After ITA: submit PR application

If invited, follow the personalised checklist and normally submit the complete electronic PR application within 60 days. Upload civil identity records, ECA, IELTS, work evidence, funds, police certificates, translations and forms. Pay IRCC fees. Do not submit incomplete or inconsistent employment claims.

IRCC apply after invitation ↗
12

Medical, police certificates, biometrics and decision

Complete the immigration medical exam with an IRCC panel physician as instructed. Obtain police certificates according to IRCC country-specific rules (usually for countries where she lived six months in a row or longer since age 18). Give biometrics when requested. Respond promptly to additional document requests and monitor the application online.

Police certificates ↗ · Medical exam ↗ · Biometrics ↗
13

PR approval and landing

Follow the Confirmation of Permanent Residence and visa instructions. Check expiry dates carefully, travel before expiry, complete landing formalities and provide a Canadian mailing address for the PR card. Arrange housing, health coverage, bank account, Social Insurance Number and job search. Budget for flights and initial living expenses separately.

12 · Important safeguards

  • Recheck live IRCC requirements, funds and fees immediately before filing. Government rules can change.
  • CRS rules changed on November 3, 2026. Do not rely on old CRS estimates; use the current official calculator/profile.
  • Never invent or exaggerate duties, hours, salary or dates. Misrepresentation can lead to refusal and a ban.
  • Do not assume “Homeroom Teacher” equals NOC 41220. Confirm the grade level and actual duties first.
  • Teaching certification is provincial. PR does not automatically grant a licence to teach.
  • Paid representation is optional. Verify the representative and get a written retainer agreement.
